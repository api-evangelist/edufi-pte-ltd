EduFi Financial Services Private Limited has partnered with University of Chenab to expand access to higher education through its Study Now, Pay Later model. The collaboration enables students to enroll with flexible, digital-first financing solutions that remove immediate tuition barriers and promote inclusive academic opportunities.
