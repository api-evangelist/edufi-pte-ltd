EduFi Financial Services Pvt Ltd has signed a strategic partnership with BAG X to introduce its Corporate Education Financing Program, offering flexible installment plans that make higher education and professional development more accessible. The collaboration reflects both organizations' commitment to building a knowledge-driven, future-ready workforce across Pakistan.
